🎯 Band 7.5–8.0: think, don't recite

Part 1: Answer the exact question first, then add one vivid detail or a small contrast. Part 2: Build a scene, explain a turning point, and reflect personally. Part 3: Take a position → explain why → compare another situation or viewpoint → give a specific example → qualify your claim. Useful expressions work only when their meanings genuinely fit; avoid forcing idioms into every answer.
